How Setu Works

1. Safe Information Bridge: Setu translates complex Indian government healthcare initiatives (Ayushman Bharat, ABHA, eSanjeevani, Ayushman Arogya Mandir) into simple, low-literacy-friendly guidance in English and Hindi.

2. Medicine Scanner & Safety Cross-Check: Use your phone camera or upload a strip image to identify medicines via client-side OCR. Setu immediately cross-checks the medicine against your recorded allergies and conditions for potential conflicts.

3. Live GPS & Smart Emergency Escalation: Calculates Haversine distances to nearest emergency hospitals with live ETA, a 10s calm countdown ring, 108/112 quick calling, and a one-tap WhatsApp family alert.

4. Consult Brief Card: Formats your symptoms, severity, and top 5 doctor questions onto an elegant 30-second sheet with a camera-scannable QR code.

5. Privacy Guaranteed: No personal or medical data is sent to external cloud servers; everything runs locally in your browser.
